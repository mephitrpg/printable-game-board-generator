About

This tool was created for printing paper A3 square game boards, compatible with the following widely available 32 cm (12.6 in) magnetic chessboard. Some models also include Backgammon.

To make an A3 foldable square board from two A4 sheets, cut away the joining margin from one sheet. Fold the corresponding margin on the other sheet to form a tab, then glue the trimmed sheet to it. The finished board fits in a plastic sleeve in a ring binder.

You can even protect your printed boards with plastic sheets available at most stationery shops. Note that laser prints are more prone to scratching.

LEXIBOOK sells a well-equipped set containing many additional components. Amazon.com - Amazon.it

To complete the kit, I also recommend this magnetic Reversi/Othello set solely to obtain plenty of additional pieces. Given the size of its pieces, a square A3 board generated by this web app can accommodate up to 11 x 11 cells, the size of the Hnefatafl game board.

It measures 25 cm (9.84 in), and the pieces measure 23.7 mm in diameter and 6 mm in height.

Of course, you can also make the pieces yourself. For example, you can use 19 mm coin capsules with 10 mm × 2 mm neodymium magnets and a 0.75 in circle punch. The 19 mm coin capsules have nearly the same external dimensions as the pieces in the Reversi/Othello set: 23.4 mm in diameter and 4.9 mm in height.

You can also buy other magnetic sets, such as Go, Shogi, Xiangqi, and more.

For games with boards larger than 11 × 11 cells, such as Halma, Chinese Checkers, Go or Hex, you’ll need to use smaller pieces, like Go stones, chess pawns, travel games' magnetic pieces or 8 mm cubes.
