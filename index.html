About

This tool was created for printing paper game boards. Both A4 and A3 paper sizes are compatible with the following widely available 32cm (12.6in) magnetic chessboard. Some models also include Backgammon.

One A3 sheet, or two A4 sheets, fit the chessboard’s size. You can protect your printed boards with plastic sheets available at most stationery shops. Laser prints are more prone to scratching.

LEXIBOOK sells a lovely set containing many additional components:

  • Italy – Lexibook chessboard
  • United States – Lexibook chessboard

To complete the kit, I also recommend this magnetic Reversi/Othello set, which provides extra pieces for even more games. Its size is 25mm (9.84in), and pieces measure 23.7mm in diameter and 6mm in height.

You can also buy other magnetic sets (Shogi, Go, Xiangqi, and more), or make your own using in example 19mm coin capsules with 10mm x 2mm neodymium magnets and a 0.75in circle cutter.
